How to read this report

Methodology: your starting point (“baseline”) is the average of the first 10 searches we ran for your business — that is the initial grid above. “Now” is the average of the most recent 10 searches, matching the latest full day of checking — that is the current grid. Both grids are overlaid on the same OpenStreetMap view of your service area. Rankings are checked from real locations in your service area, on the dates shown. Places not yet checked are left hatched — they are never estimated. This report covers only the period since your campaign started; nothing before it is reconstructed.

Questions? Reply to the email this report arrived with — your agency sees the same numbers you do.
